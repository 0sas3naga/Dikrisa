About Dikrisa International School

“A school where your child is known, cared for, and prepared for the future.”

Parents in Abuja choose Dikrisa because we combine structured academics with genuine warmth and strong moral upbringing. Every child receives attentive guidance in a calm, well-organized learning environment from their earliest years through secondary school.

Academic Stages

Programs From Creche to Secondary

Four carefully structured stages that grow with your child under one trusted school roof in Abuja.

Ages 3 Months – 2 Years 01

Creche

Loving, hygienic daytime care for infants and toddlers with warm caregivers, sensory play, gentle routines, and safe rest areas.

Ages 2 – 5 Years 02

Nursery

Phonics-based early reading, number sense, creative art, and social confidence built through joyful, hands-on classroom discovery.

Primary 1 – 6 03

Primary

Solid mastery of English, Mathematics, Basic Science, ICT, and moral values in small classes where no pupil is left behind.

JSS 1 – SSS 3 04

Secondary

Rigorous Junior and Senior Secondary learning focused on Sciences, Arts, Commercial subjects, leadership, and WAEC/NECO readiness.
